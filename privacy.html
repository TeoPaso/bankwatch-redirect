Bank Watch - Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Bank Watch is a personal, non-commercial budgeting app used by a single individual (its author) on his own phone, to read his own bank accounts.

What data is processed

  • Balances and transactions of the owner's own bank accounts, obtained through the Enable Banking API after the owner gives consent to his bank.
  • Technical data needed to call the API (an application identifier and a cryptographic key kept in the phone's secure hardware).

Where the data is stored

Only on the owner's phone. There is no server operated by Bank Watch, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party sharing. Data is not sent anywhere other than to Enable Banking and to the owner's bank, as required to read the accounts.

This website

This site only hosts static pages. The return page shown after the bank authorisation does not store, log or transmit the parameters it receives in its address; they stay in the owner's browser and are pasted into the app by the owner.

Retention and withdrawal

The owner can revoke the consent at any time from the app, from Enable Banking or from his bank, and can delete all data by uninstalling the app.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the issue tracker of this GitHub repository.
